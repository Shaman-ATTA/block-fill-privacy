Privacy Policy — Crownless

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy describes how the Android game Crownless (package com.attacorp.crownless, the "App") handles information. The App is developed by ATTA CORP ("we").

1. Summary

  • The App works fully offline. It does not request internet access or any device permissions.
  • We do not operate our own servers and we do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.
  • The App shows no ads and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and has no in-app purchases.

2. Data stored on your device

Your campaign saves and settings (such as the chosen language and sound volume) are stored only in the App's private storage on your device. They are never transmitted to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes them.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help address it.

Your rights and data deletion

Because we do not hold any personal data, there is nothing for us to access, export or delete. Data held by Google services can be managed in your Google Account (myaccount.google.com). Local data is removed by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
