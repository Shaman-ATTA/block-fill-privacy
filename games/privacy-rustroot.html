Privacy Policy — Rustroot

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy describes how the Android game Rustroot (package com.attacorp.rustroot, the "App") handles information. The App is developed by ATTA CORP ("we").

1. Summary

  • We do not operate our own servers and we do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.
  • The App shows no ads and contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • Your colonies, saves and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • The App can use two Google services: Google Play Billing (optional one-time full unlock) and Google Play Games Services (optional sign-in, achievements and cloud saves).

2. In-app purchase (Google Play Billing)

The optional one-time full unlock is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The App only receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play to unlock the full game and restore it on reinstall. See the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Google Play Games Services

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the App may use Play Games Services for achievements and Saved Games (a cloud copy of your save). Google processes your Play Games player ID, gamer name/avatar and game activity under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your email address or contacts.

4. Data stored on your device

Saves and settings are kept in the App's private storage. If you choose to export or share a save file, it goes only where you send it using Android's own file picker or share sheet. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes local saves.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help address it.

Your rights and data deletion

Because we do not hold any personal data, there is nothing for us to access, export or delete. Data held by Google services can be managed in your Google Account (myaccount.google.com). Local data is removed by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
