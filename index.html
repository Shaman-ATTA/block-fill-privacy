Privacy Policy — Block Fill: One Line Puzzle

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy describes how the Android game Block Fill: One Line Puzzle (package com.blockfill.puzzle.offline, the "App") handles information. The App is developed by an independent developer ("we").

1. Summary

  • We do not operate our own servers and we do not collect, store, or sell any personal data ourselves.
  • Your game progress (completed levels, hints, daily puzzle streak, settings, chosen theme) is stored locally on your device only.
  • The App uses Google services: Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), Google Play Games Services, and Google Play Billing. These services may collect data as described below and in Google's own policies.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To serve, measure and personalize ads, and for analytics and fraud prevention, the SDK may collect and process:

  • the Android advertising ID (and app set ID);
  • device information (model, OS version, language, screen size) and approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • IP address;
  • ad interaction data (ad impressions, taps) and diagnostic data (crash/performance data of the SDK).

This data is sent to and processed by Google, encrypted in transit. Where required (e.g. in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you will be asked for consent via Google's consent message. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads / Privacy > Ads). See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

You can remove ads with the one-time "Remove ads" in-app purchase.

3. Google Play Games Services

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the App uses Play Games Services for sign-in, achievements and leaderboards. Google processes your Play Games player ID, gamer name/avatar and game activity (e.g. achievements, scores). This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your email address or contacts.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The App only receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play to unlock the feature and to restore it on reinstall.

5. Data stored on your device

Progress and settings are saved in the App's private storage on your device. They are not transmitted to us. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes this information. You can also reset progress in the App's Settings.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help address it.

7. Your rights and data deletion

Because we do not hold any personal data, we cannot access, export or delete data held by Google. To manage data collected by Google services, use your Google Account settings (myaccount.google.com) and the ad settings on your device. Local data is removed by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage.

8. Security

Data sent by the Google SDKs is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
